Privacy Policy for Thai Power Cost

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Thai Power Cost is a simple electricity-cost calculator. We respect your privacy.

Data Collection

Your calculator inputs, including the optional device name, power, usage hours, days, electricity rate, and calculated results, are processed locally on your device. We do not send these values to Google Analytics or as advertising parameters.

Versions of Thai Power Cost that include Google Analytics for Firebase collect app usage information, such as first opens, sessions, screen views, and calculation-button events. Analytics also processes an app-instance identifier, device and app information (such as device model, operating system, app version, and language), and approximate location derived from IP addresses. The app does not request GPS or precise-location access.

We use this information to understand app usage and improve the app. Analytics collection is automatic in these versions; the app currently has no in-app analytics opt-out. Earlier versions without Analytics do not send these usage events.

Third Parties

We use Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google, to process analytics information. Analytics data is encrypted in transit using TLS and may be processed on Google infrastructure outside your country. See Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security information.

Versions with advertising use Google AdMob to display a banner on the results screen. The advertising SDK may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, performance and diagnostic information, and device identifiers such as the app set ID with Google and its advertising partners for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit. This app requests non-personalized ads, disables Android Advertising ID collection, and does not request GPS or precise-location permission. Non-personalized ads can still process data for these purposes.

Google's User Messaging Platform manages advertising privacy messages where required. Advertising requests are made only when the platform reports that ads may be requested. Where required, the app shows an Ad privacy choices control so you can manage or withdraw your choices. If consent cannot be established, the calculator remains usable without loading ads. Privacy choices for ads do not switch off the separate Firebase Analytics collection described above.

See How Google uses information for advertising and How Google uses information from partner apps. Earlier versions without advertising do not load ads. The app has no user accounts or cloud storage for calculator inputs. We do not sell user data or send calculator inputs as analytics parameters.

Retention and Requests

Analytics data is retained according to the Google Analytics property's configured retention settings and Google's applicable retention rules. We use aggregate reports to understand usage. Contact us at the email below with privacy or deletion questions. Because the app has no user account and we do not associate Analytics identifiers with your name or email, we may be unable to identify a particular installation's analytics records from an email address alone.

Advertising and consent data are retained by Google and its partners according to their policies and applicable requirements. Uninstalling the app stops future collection from that installation but does not automatically delete previously collected Analytics or advertising data.

Children's Privacy

Thai Power Cost is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed at children. The optional appliance name is not a request for your personal name. We do not request your age, contact information, or account registration. Ads are requested with Google's G content-rating limit. If you have concerns about a child's data, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same public URL.
